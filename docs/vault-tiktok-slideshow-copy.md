# VAULT — TikTok Slideshow Copy

**Status:** Pre-research draft. Written from positioning + category knowledge, not from verbatim voice-of-customer data. Hooks should be revisited once the Deep Research returns real quotes.

---

## Format rules these follow

- **Slide 1 is 80% of performance.** It must be readable in under one second and land a pain point, not a product.
- **One idea per slide.** Max ~12 words per slide, fewer is better.
- **6–9 slides.** Long enough to build tension, short enough to finish.
- **No product until slide 4 at the earliest.** Lead with the wound.
- **Second person, present tense.** "You" not "people."
- **Last slide is the ask.**

## Compliance guardrails baked in

- All dollar figures are framed as examples, never promises. Any slide showing a number carries **"Illustrative example"** on-screen.
- No "financial advice," no guaranteed returns, no "we'll make you money."
- Bank access described as **read-only via Plaid** wherever the connection is mentioned.
- Avoid conspiracy-toned claims about banks. "Your bank doesn't tell you" is fine. "Banks are hiding this from you" is not.

# Production notes

- **Test hooks, not slideshows.** Run one body with 3–4 different slide 1s before building new concepts.
- **Slideshows 4, 8, and 10** are the only three that sell the tier system and Wealth Velocity Score. Those are your differentiators and they're underweighted here on purpose — pain first, mechanism second. If early testing shows the status angle pulling, build 3 more in that lane.
- **Slideshow 5 and 8** are the most likely organic performers. Slideshow 1 and 6 are the most likely paid performers.
- Every slideshow that names a dollar figure needs the illustrative-example disclaimer on-screen, not just in the caption.
